# One brand colour, a readable palette

Dastaan takes `brand.color` and solves the rest: a light page tinted by the colour, ink dark enough to read on it,
a night ground for dark scenes, and the accent itself, each pair checked for contrast in every frame. That is why
the founder chooses one colour, not a palette.

- **Greys, near-white and near-black are not brand colours**: they are a page. `dastaan_brand` leaves them out.
- **A very pale or very dark brand colour** still works: it becomes the accent or the night, and the check refuses
  any pair that would not read. If a scene's contrast fails, the finding names it; try their second colour.
- **Two colours** (a logo in orange and teal): ask which one they want people to remember. The other stays in the
  logo itself, which Dastaan shows as given.
- **The logo** is shown exactly as supplied (PNG, JPEG, WebP or SVG). A logo with a transparent background reads best;
  a white logo needs a dark scene.
