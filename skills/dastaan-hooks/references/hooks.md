# Hooks: what the research says, and what Dastaan holds you to

From the team's hook research (bus memory `doc/dastaan-hook-research-20261108`; sources in the engine's
`scene-pack/GUIDE.md`).

| Type | What it is | From a founder's facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Value** | Lead with the figure or the specific result | `48 hours.` / `20% off this weekend.` |
| **Desire** | The outcome the viewer wants | `Your 3D site model in 48 hours.` |
| **Loss** | Their problem or wait, asked as a question | `Still waiting weeks for site drawings?` (only if they named the wait) |
| **Information** | Open a gap the next scene answers | `Most site surveys take weeks. Ours doesn't.` |

- Social proof only when the founder gave the proof (a customer's words, a count).
- The opening is never a logo or a greeting, and it is on screen at frame 0 (Dastaan's gate).
- Hook, then story or value, then proof, then the offer. A bait hook the video does not pay off raises skips later.
- Hooks fatigue: offer the founder a different type, not a rewording of the same one.
- At most 8 words. Every word is the founder's own (Dastaan checks each one and names any that is not).

When a hook comes back with "not in their words", ask the founder whether it is true and get it in their words
(then add those words with `dastaan_edit {id, brief}`), or drop it.
