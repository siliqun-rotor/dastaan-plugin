# Looking at the frame sheet

`dastaan_look` saves one PNG per language and size: a row per scene, four frames each (a quarter, half, three
quarters, settled end). The gates already hold the format, the safe frame, overlaps, contrast and length. Your eyes
check what they cannot:

- **The opening.** Is the hook on screen at once and readable in a second? Is it the strongest fact the founder
  gave? If not, swap a hook (`dastaan_hooks`).
- **One idea per scene.** A scene that needs reading twice is two scenes, or one line too many.
- **Order.** Hook, then what it is, then the proof they gave, then their offer or action.
- **Arabic.** Look at the `ar` sheet too: it reads right to left, and lines wrap sooner.
- **Every size you deliver.** Look at `--size 1:1` and `16:9` when you render them; a scene that breathes in 9:16 can
  crowd in 16:9.
- **Numbers.** Each figure is one the founder gave, with its unit and its condition ("4 classes a month").

Then render. Tell the founder which files you made, their sizes and languages, and that the fingerprint identifies
each render.
